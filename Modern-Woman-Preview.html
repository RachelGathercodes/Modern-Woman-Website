01 / THE BAND

A legendary songbook.
A live-wire tribute.

Some music just brings people together.

Modern Woman–The All-Female Billy Joel Tribute Experience–rocks out in tribute to one of the world's biggest, most relevant, highest quality, and most beloved music acts.

Get ready to sing and dance to some of the best and most familiar tunes in pop and rock history, in a fun, energetic, and unique tribute show!

Based in Durham, North Carolina, Modern Woman is comprised of award-winning and multiple award-nominated musicians.
